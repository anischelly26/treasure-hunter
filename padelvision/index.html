      03 / ENGINEERING

      Two players.
      Clear evidence.

      MediaPipe estimates body landmarks for up to four visible players. The app selects two on your chosen side, compares estimated foot positions across sampled frames and shows both skeletons. Position and drill suggestions use documented rules; a coach-labelled training set is still needed for a learned strategy model.

      PROTOTYPE / LIMITATIONS

      This is a positional review from one camera, not a calibrated court tracking system. Pair identification can switch during occlusion or crossing. A near-net frame does not prove that a volley happened; deeper play does not prove a defensive shot. The app does not detect faults, shot outcomes or an optimal style. Its tactical rules need coach validation and labelled rallies before a strategy model can be trained or evaluated.
