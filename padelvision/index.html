      RESEARCH PROTOTYPE / LIMITATIONS

      The phases divide time evenly; they do not identify the actual impact frame. A body-pose model cannot track the racket or ball. Camera angle and occlusion affect measurements. Coaching reference labels in the Python project still require validation by qualified coaches. No accuracy or performance grade is claimed.
