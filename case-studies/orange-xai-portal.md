# MISSION_02 — Internship & PFE Management Portal with Explainable AI Shortlisting

**Context:** Orange Tunisia (Orange Digital Center) × MedTech  
**Type:** Senior team project, 2025–2026  
**Team:** Asma Gannar, Eya Bedoui, Fares Anas, Adem Bouaccida, Skander Bouricha, Anis Chelly

## Problem

Internship and PFE recruitment can become slow, fragmented and difficult to justify when coordinators manually process large numbers of CVs. The project targeted both operational efficiency and transparency: automate the workflow without turning candidate ranking into an unexplained black box.

## Solution

The team designed a centralized full-stack portal supporting the internship lifecycle for coordinators, students and company supervisors.

Core scope included:

- internship / PFE offer publication
- student applications and CV upload
- CV parsing and skill extraction
- skill-to-offer matching
- candidate ranking and recommendation
- human-readable explanations for shortlisting decisions
- supervisor assignment
- milestone / workflow tracking
- evaluation management

## Engineering direction

The project specification was designed around a modern web architecture using:

- React
- Node.js
- Express
- MongoDB
- AI matching / explainability components
- Microsoft Azure deployment environment
- Agile / Scrum delivery

The AI module is positioned as **decision support**, not as an autonomous final decision-maker.

## Why it matters

The interesting engineering problem is not simply ranking CVs. It is producing a ranking that a coordinator can inspect and explain. That changes the system from a black-box scorer into an auditable decision-support workflow.
